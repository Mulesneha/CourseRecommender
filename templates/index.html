        The course map

        Every dot is a course. Courses that teach similar things sit close together, and KMeans groups them into eight learning paths. Your picks glow yellow.

          How the picks are made

          Content-based

          Each course becomes a TF-IDF vector from its title, skills and description. Your interests and liked courses become a vector too, and cosine similarity ranks the closest courses.

          Collaborative

          From 250 students' ratings we measure how similar courses are by who liked them. If you loved one course, we predict your rating for the ones its fans also loved.

          Clustering

          TruncatedSVD compresses the TF-IDF vectors, KMeans groups them into learning paths, and PCA flattens them into the map above.

          Built with Flask, pandas and scikit-learn. Ratings in this demo are synthetic.
